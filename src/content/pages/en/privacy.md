This page explains how tariksenkal.com handles privacy and cookies. In short: this is a simple, static personal blog and it does not track you.

## What data is processed?

There are no sign-ups, forms or accounts on this site, and we do not collect your personal data. The site only handles the technical requests needed to show you its pages.

## Cookies and local storage

No advertising or tracking cookies are used. Your browser’s **local storage (localStorage)** is used only to remember:

- **Theme preference** (system / light / dark)
- **Writing page layout** (grid / list)

This data stays in your browser and is never sent to us or to third parties. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

## Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; fonts, images and the search index are served from the site itself. The hosting provider may keep access logs (such as IP addresses) for technical reasons. There are no analytics or advertising scripts. If you click a “share” link on a post, that platform’s own privacy terms (X, LinkedIn) apply.
