Hello, I'm **İsmail Tarık Şenkal**, Tarık for short. I build companies in AI and software, and today I'm one of the co-founders of **HatsonTech** and **Caseon AI**. In this post I wanted to briefly introduce myself and the road so far.

## In brief

-   **Education:** Bachelor's, Hacettepe University (2018–2023); master's in Statistics, METU (2026–present).
-   **Companies:** HatsonTech (Hatson Teknoloji Ltd. Şti.) and Caseon AI (Caseon Teknoloji A.Ş.), co-founder.
-   **Interests:** Large language models, natural language processing, RAG, legal technology and entrepreneurship.
-   **Based in:** Ankara.

## Education

I completed my bachelor's degree at Hacettepe University between 2018 and 2023. Since 2026 I have been doing a master's in Statistics at METU.

## Our first company

In 2024, Halide Yılmaz and I founded our first company, in AI. In early 2025 we took part in large-scale model training work through TÜBİTAK events, Türkiye's national high-performance computing infrastructure **TRUBA**, and the European **EUMaster4HPC** programme.

The same year, our legal-software project took us into METU Technopark, and in 2026 the project received investment. In July 2026 we sold the project to another company. That journey taught me the most about shipping a product from scratch, building a team, talking with customers and running a company day to day.

## HatsonTech and Caseon AI

In August 2026 we founded our main company, **HatsonTech**, in Ankara. At HatsonTech we run projects for organisations on AI agents, source-citing RAG assistants, language-model fine-tuning, computer vision, data engineering, and web and mobile software. Our aim is to build long-term partnerships with organisations rather than one-off jobs.

Also in 2026, we founded **Caseon AI** as a separate company for contract analysis. At Caseon we are building software that helps lawyers with due diligence and contract review. Every answer the system gives is shown with the document paragraph it relies on, and the final check always stays with the lawyer.

Since 2024, together with our earlier work, we have been building technology products in environment, law, software and AI. Today I spend my time on the growth, products and teams of these two companies.

## A few things I care about

-   **The team.** Every piece of work mentioned here is the effort of the people I work with.
-   **Showing the source.** An answer means more when you can see where it came from; in fields like law, this is a requirement rather than a preference.
-   **Thinking long term.** Growing a company and maturing a product both take time; we would rather move in steady, solid steps.
-   **Writing as I learn.** This blog is partly notes to myself. If I get something wrong, I'm glad to be corrected.
